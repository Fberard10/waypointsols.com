Who We Are

Waypoint Solutions LLC is an Albuquerque-based U.S. small business built to supply federal agencies with fasteners and industrial hardware. We source against DLA/DIBBS solicitations — bolts, nuts, screws, washers, pins, rivets, gaskets, and specialty hardware — screening every requirement for sourcing restrictions, export controls, and price history before we ever quote. Founded by Federico Berard and Braden Cote, Waypoint brings finance-educated leadership and disciplined sourcing oversight to every solicitation we pursue.

Our Sourcing Process

Every solicitation Waypoint pursues moves through the same four checkpoints before a quote reaches a contracting officer.

Step Checkpoint What We Verify
01 — Screen Solicitation Screening Sourcing type (open-market vs. restricted-source), price history, and required quantity against a GO/NO-GO framework.
02 — Verify Compliance Verification Export control gates (ITAR/JCP), Nonmanufacturer Rule, Buy American/DFARS specialty metals, and obsolete/superseded NSN checks.
03 — Source Supplier Sourcing Quotes built only from verified manufacturers and distributors, with packaging and inspection matched to MIL-STD-2073/129 as required.
04 — Deliver Quote & Delivery Direct CO/COR communication from quote through delivery, with lead time and quantity tracked against the award.
