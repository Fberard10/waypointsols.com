HOW WE OPERATE

Our Sourcing Process

Every solicitation moves through the same four checkpoints before a quote reaches a contracting officer — no shortcuts, no guesswork on sourcing restrictions or export controls.

01 — SCREEN

Solicitation Screening

Every NSN is run through a GO/NO-GO framework — sourcing type, price history, and quantity checked before we commit time to a quote.

02 — VERIFY

Compliance Verification

Export control gates, restricted-source flags, and Nonmanufacturer Rule requirements confirmed before pursuit — not after award.

03 — SOURCE

Supplier Sourcing

Quotes built only from verified manufacturers and distributors, with packaging and inspection matched to the solicitation's requirements.

04 — DELIVER

Quote & Delivery

Direct CO/COR communication from quote through delivery, with full accountability for lead time and quantity as awarded.
